How the authoring-path finding was verified

30 sampled authoring paths were requested. All 30 returned HTTP 200 with text/html and a final address of /utils/404?item=..., the site's soft-404 page. A link checker that reads status codes alone would score them as healthy. Public copies of documents are served from the media handler path instead, for example /-/media/dotgov/files/..., and mapping an authoring path to its public address is not a mechanical substitution: 10 sampled items could not be resolved that way. The count of documents that are public and must stay public therefore comes from the District's media export, not from the sitemap.

Permit process

Permit Types and Process

The Air District's permitting process encompasses multiple permit types to ensure comprehensive environmental compliance. While most projects require two fundamental permits, certain major facilities are subject to additional regulatory oversight.

The standard permitting process requires two critical permits that must be obtained prior to initiating any project. The two essential permits are the Authority to Construct (ATC) and the Permit to Operate (PO).

During a live virtual press conference from Antalya on November 10, Air District leaders described how regional action protects communities already living with the impacts of climate change.

At COP31, Air District representatives joined subnational and regional leaders to discuss resilient cities, electrification and grids and sustainable transportation.
