How the authoring-path finding was proven

30 randomly selected /sitecore/shell/sitecore/media-library/… URLs were requested. All 30 returned HTTP 200 with text/html and a final URL of /utils/404?item=… — the site's soft-404 page. Amusingly, that means they look "fine" to a link checker that only reads status codes. Public documents are served from a different path, /-/media/dotgov/files/…; 45 of 45 sampled document links returned application/pdf.

News release

Air District shares Bay Area clean air leadership at COP31 in Antalya

The Air District shared how the Bay Area turns climate commitments into cleaner air at the United Nations Climate Change Conference, or COP31, which runs November 9 through 20. During a live virtual press conference from Antalya on November 10, Air District leaders described how regional action protects communities already living with the impacts of climate change.

At COP31, Air District representatives joined subnational and regional leaders to discuss resilient cities, electrification and grids and sustainable transportation. The Air District emphasized its work to deliver cleaner air at the regional level through programs and funding opportunities, while working with partners at COP31 to accelerate solutions for the people who need them most.

Agricultural Waste Chipping Program

Thank you for taking advantage of the Air District’s Agricultural Waste Chipping Program, and for keeping unhealthy smoke emissions from Bay Area skies. Your feedback allows us to measure and improve the level of service we provide to our customers, and it helps ensure program funds are used effectively to protect public health and the environment.

Program

Permit Types and Process

The Air District's permitting process encompasses multiple permit types to ensure comprehensive environmental compliance. While most projects require two fundamental permits, certain major facilities are subject to additional regulatory oversight.

The standard permitting process requires two critical permits that must be obtained prior to initiating any project. The two essential permits are the Authority to Construct (ATC) and the Permit to Operate (PO).
